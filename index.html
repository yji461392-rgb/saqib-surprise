A letter from the heart

Dear Saqib, ❤️

Kabhi-kabhi zindagi mein kuch sapne sirf sapne nahi hote. Woh hamari pehchaan ban jaate hain. Aur mujhe pata hai ki tumhara sapna bhi tumhare liye kuch aisa hi hai.

Saqib, tum ek din doctor banna chahte ho. AIIMS tak pahunchna chahte ho. Ye raasta aasaan nahi hai, lekin mushkil hone ka matlab ye nahi ki tum ise poora nahi kar sakte.

Ho sakta hai kuch din padhai bahut achhi ho. Ho sakta hai kuch din questions solve na hon, marks kam aayein ya confidence hil jaaye. Lekin ek test tumhari poori kahani decide nahi karta. Galtiyan tumhe sikhane aati hain, tumhe rokne nahi.

Jab doosre log aage nikalte hue dikhein, apni journey ko unse compare mat karna. Tumhari race tumhari apni hai. Har din thoda seekhna, apni galtiyon ko samajhna aur zaroorat padne par help maangna bhi progress hai.

Yaad rakhna: bade results sirf bade promises se nahi, balki roz ke chhote aur imaandaar efforts se bante hain. Aaj ka ek chapter, aaj ke kuch questions aur aaj ki ek nayi learning — ye sab matter karte hain.

Aur Saqib, tumhari value sirf marks ya rank se nahi hai. Apni health ka khayal rakhna, sahi neend lena, breaks lena aur zaroorat par kisi se baat karna bhi utna hi important hai. Strong banna matlab khud par bekaar ka pressure daalna nahi; strong banna matlab dobara koshish karna seekhna hai.

Jis din thak jao, apne sapne ko yaad karna. Lekin sirf sapna nahi — woh wajah bhi yaad karna jiski wajah se tumne shuru kiya tha.

Mujhe umeed hai ki ek din tum apni mehnat par peeche mudkar muskuraoge aur kahoge: “Achha hua, maine koshish karna nahi chhoda.”

Abhi tum ek student ho, ek learner ho, aur tumhari kahani abhi likhi ja rahi hai. Aage kya hoga, koi guarantee nahi de sakta — lekin aaj tum apna agla step zaroor le sakte ho.

Keep learning. Keep going. Apne sapne ke liye mehnat karo, ek din mein ek step.

Dil se best wishes, Saqib. ❤️
